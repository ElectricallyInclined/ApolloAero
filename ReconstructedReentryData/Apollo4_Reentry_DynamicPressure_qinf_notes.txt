NASA TN D-5399, Apollo 4 entry aerodynamics. PDF page 46 (printed page 38), Figure 8(a), dynamic pressure.

This file traces only the solid q_inf curve. The separate dashed q_inf,p curve was omitted at the user's request.

Method: WebPlotDigitizer calibrated linear XY axes; 54 manually selected points along the solid curve, including its two peaks. No smoothing, fitting or uniform-time resampling. Calibration anchors: 29960 s and 30760 s horizontally, 0 and 560 lb/ft^2 vertically. Ground elapsed time is plotted in seconds.

Approximate scan digitization, not original flight measurements. The dense grid, curve thickness and scan quality limit accuracy. At tracing scale, one image pixel represented about 1.5 s horizontally and 1.5 lb/ft^2 vertically; errors can exceed one pixel. The 0.1 decimals preserve selected coordinates and do not claim measurement precision. The chart also has a printed note about flight-measured pressure exceeding 5 percent of full scale; use the report for that qualification.
