NASA TN D-5399, Apollo 4 entry aerodynamics. PDF page 45 (printed page 37), Figure 7(c), deceleration load factor L_f.

This is a denser image-assisted trace of the single plotted curve. The original headerless Apollo4_Reentry_GLoads.csv (77 points) remains unchanged for comparison. Visual overlay confirms that its 77 points closely follow Figure 7(c); they were used to define a narrow search corridor for the new pixel trace. The new 312 points were sampled along the rotated 2.5x PDF image at roughly 2-4-second intervals, with original feature anchors retained; pixels were selected with a curve-aligned darkness score. The overlay (Figure7c_GLoads_dense_overlay.png) beside this CSV shows the resulting trace against the source scan. No smoothing or physical model was applied.

The horizontal axis is ground elapsed time in seconds; the vertical axis is deceleration load factor in g. Approximate scan-derived values, not original measurements. Dense horizontal grid lines, curve thickness and scan distortion limit accuracy; errors may exceed a pixel (about 0.64 s horizontally and 0.008 g vertically at rendering scale).
