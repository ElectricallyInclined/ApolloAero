Figure 4 concerns configuration C at c.g. x/d = -0.685, z/d = 0.059 (caption on preceding panel). The six curves are Mach 0.4, 0.7, 0.9, 1.1, 1.2 and 1.35. Legend Reynolds numbers R x 10^-6 are 1.078 for Mach 0.4 through 1.2 and 0.924 for Mach 1.35.

Critical calibration: all curves share angle of attack and one coefficient scale, but each Mach trace has its own displayed zero origin. On the 2.5x rendered PDF image, alpha=0 deg is x=369.5 and alpha=200 deg is x=1214. The 0.05 coefficient increment spans 85 pixels. Local zero lines (top to bottom Mach order) are image y=402, 573, 744, 909, 1082 and 1245. Each CSV coefficient is calculated relative to its own zero line; vertical stacking offsets are not data.

Method: 269 points. Manual curve guides separate the six plots; a grid-suppressed, curve-aligned image-darkness search selects pixels near each guide. Broad portions are sampled every 5 deg, with denser points around sharp transitions. The colored source overlay (ApolloCM_CmCG_Figure4b_overlay.png) and reproducible extraction script (trace_ApolloCM_CmCG_Figure4b.py) are beside the PDF and CSV. The M=0.4 near-vertical printed drop at about alpha=70 deg is represented by before_drop at 70.0 deg and after_drop at 70.1 deg; the 0.1-deg width is a plotting convention, not a measured transition width.

All values are approximate scan digitizations, not original wind-tunnel measurements. Dense grid, symbols and dashed portions increase uncertainty; plotted decimal places preserve calibration coordinates, not measurement accuracy.
