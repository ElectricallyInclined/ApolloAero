NASA TN D-4688, Aerodynamic Stability Characteristics of the Apollo Command Module (August 1968). Figure 5(b), printed page 49 / PDF page 62, pitching-moment coefficient about the center of gravity, C_m,c.g. Figure 5 caption on the preceding page identifies Apollo command module configuration C, c.g. x/d=-0.685 and z/d=0.059.

Five series are plotted: Mach 1.575 (R x 10^-6=1.16, NAA-SAL), 1.65 (0.724, JPL-20 SWT), 2.41 (0.724, JPL-20 SWT), 2.49 (0.79, NAA-SAL) and 3.27 (0.54, NAA-SAL). The R values and facility labels are transcribed from the graph legend.

Critical calibration: all five curves share angle of attack and coefficient increment but have separate displayed zero origins. On the upright 2.5x PDF render, alpha=0 deg is x=617.5 and alpha=200 deg is x=1518. The coefficient increment 0.05 spans 90 pixels. Zero lines for Mach 1.575, 1.65, 2.41, 2.49 and 3.27 respectively are image y=303, 484, 574, 664 and 753.5. Each CSV coefficient is calculated relative to its own zero line; the vertical stacking offsets are not data.

Method: 208 points across the five curves, sampled mostly at 5-degree angle intervals. Manually placed curve guides separate the series; a grid-suppressed, curve-aligned image-darkness search selects pixels close to each guide. The colored source overlay (ApolloCM_CmCG_Figure5b_overlay.png) and reproducible script (trace_ApolloCM_CmCG_Figure5b.py) are beside the PDF and CSV. The traced lines follow the plotted curves rather than isolated off-curve symbols.

These are approximate scan digitizations, not original wind-tunnel measurements. The dense grid, printed symbols and faint line segments limit precision; CSV decimals preserve plotted coordinates, not experimental accuracy.
